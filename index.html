NASA / JPL · Deep Space Network

DSN Now

Earth antennas communicating with spacecraft across the Solar System. Uplink sends signals to spacecraft; downlink receives them. An active signal does not necessarily carry science data.

Open this panel to request NASA station data.

Refreshes every 15 seconds while this panel and browser tab are visible. Source data older than two minutes is marked stale. Inactive signals may include old data rates, which are not displayed as current traffic. Missing antennas or signals do not establish mission health. The Mars filter includes known Mars mission codes, including cruise missions.
